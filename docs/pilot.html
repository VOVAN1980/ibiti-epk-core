Straight answers

Questions we get.

Is EPK v2 audited?

Not yet. EPK has 202 automated tests (contracts, Watchtower and SDK), static analysis and four internal security reviews, and an independent audit comes before mainnet. Until then: testnet only.

Can IBITI Labs touch my vault?

No. You own your vault. Nobody — including us — can upgrade it, change its rules or move its funds for you. New versions are opt-in through your own timelock.

What if the Watchtower's key is stolen?

A guardian can only cancel pending changes or press panic. It can never move funds. The worst it can do is pause the vault, which you lift through the timelock.

Which chains?

BSC Testnet today. The vault is a standard EVM contract; more networks are on the roadmap after the audit.
